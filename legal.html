Wallet-landia is a free, non-commercial hobby project: it shows public Ethereum wallet data as a castle and a town, and has games built on it. It has no advertising and sells nothing.

Privacy

The person named above is responsible for this site under the GDPR. There are no accounts and no cookies, and nothing you enter is kept on a server of ours.

What stays in your browser

Your Zerion key, your wallets and their names, your settings, the history of your wallets gathered so far, game progress and copies of the site's files are kept in your browser's storage (local storage, IndexedDB and the service worker's cache). This is needed for the parts of the site you use (§ 165 (3) TKG 2021), so no consent banner is shown. You can delete all of it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser; the Guild tab also has "Forget" for the key.

What leaves your browser, and to whom

  • Hosting (Vercel Inc., USA). The site is served by Vercel. Like any web host it sees your IP address, browser and the pages you load; its request logs on our plan are kept for about an hour. Vercel takes part in the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and processes this for us under its data processing agreement.
  • Wallet data (Zerion). To draw your castle, the wallet addresses you add (and wallets you visit or follow) are sent to Zerion's API with your own Zerion key. These requests pass through this site's server so that Zerion accepts them; there your key and the addresses are only forwarded, never stored or logged, and your IP address, cookies and browser details are not passed on. Zerion (Zerion Inc., USA) handles them under its privacy policy.
  • Ethereum node. For the live block bell and to watch your wallets, your browser talks directly to a public Ethereum node (by default PublicNode, publicnode.com; you can choose another in the Guild tab). The node sees your IP address and the addresses it is asked about.
  • Pictures. Protocol logos and NFT pictures come from the hosts that publish them (for example Zerion's image servers, Alchemy, OpenSea, IPFS and Arweave gateways). Some are fetched through this site's server, so the host does not see you; others are loaded by your browser directly, and then the host sees your IP address.
  • Visitor statistics (Vercel Web Analytics). We count page views to see how the site is used: page address (without anything after ? or #), referrer, country, browser, operating system and device type. It sets no cookies and stores nothing on your device; visitors are told apart by a hash that is discarded after 24 hours, and we only see totals.
  • Connect wallet. If you use it, your browser wallet tells this page your address, and that address is treated like one you typed in. No signature is ever requested.
  • Shared castles. A link you share carries the wallets you marked public in the part of the address after #, which browsers never send to a server.

Public wallet addresses can be personal data when they can be linked to a person. We do not try to find out who is behind an address.
