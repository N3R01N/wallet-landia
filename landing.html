How it works

  1. 1

    Get a free key

    Zenigard reads wallets through Zerion. Your own free key keeps what you look at between you and Zerion.

  2. 2

    Enter your wallet

    An address or an ENS name. Add more wallets later: each one raises a tower of your castle.

  3. 3

    Watch your town live

    Couriers carry your transactions to the protocols you use. Earn badges, level up, visit friends.

  • A tower for every wallet, sized by what it holds.
  • A building for every protocol: the DEXs on Market Street, lending on Bankers' Row.
  • A courier for every transaction, paying the toll (the gas) on the way.
  • The bell rings every block, so the town lives with the chain.

Questions

Can Zenigard touch my funds?

No. It only reads public data. It never asks you to connect a wallet or sign anything, and it cannot move anything.

What is a Zerion key, and is it safe to give it to Zenigard?

A free key from Zerion that lets your browser read wallet data. It is saved only in your browser and goes only to Zerion (through this site's server, which passes it on and never stores it). It can read, never spend.

Does it cost anything?

No. Zenigard is free, and Zerion's free plan covers a castle's daily needs.

Which chains does it show?

Ethereum mainnet, with your positions on the main networks Zerion tracks.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. It runs in the browser on phones, tablets and computers, and you can add it to your home screen like an app.
